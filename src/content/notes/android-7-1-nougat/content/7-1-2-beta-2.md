The second Android 7.1.2 beta came with no blog post from Google. Its build,
`NPG47I`, appears on Google's 7.1.2 public beta download page for the Nexus
5X, Nexus 6P, Pixel C, Nexus Player, Pixel and Pixel XL. The date comes from
the press: 9to5Google reported the rollout on 20 March 2017.

|                |                                                               |
| -------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Release date   | 20 March 2017, per 9to5Google; Google gave no date            |
| Build          | `NPG47I` on every device                                      |
| Devices        | Pixel, Pixel XL, Nexus 5X, Nexus 6P, Nexus Player and Pixel C |
| Security patch | March 2017, per 9to5Google                                    |

This time the Nexus 6P was in the first wave. It had received the first beta
only about three weeks earlier, 9to5Google noted. Beta 2 gave it the Pixel's
fingerprint swipe gesture, under Settings > "Moves". 9to5Google also found a
"Powered by Android" line on the Pixel boot screen. On the Pixel C it showed
the Pixel Launcher, the Pixel's white navigation buttons and a new grid of
recent apps, still unfinished: apps could not be swiped away.

Nothing changed for developers. The final 7.1.2 followed in early April 2017,
covered in the overview above.
