Android 16 is the release that made large screens non-negotiable. On tablets
and foldables, apps targeting API 36 can no longer lock orientation or refuse
to resize, and predictive back and edge-to-edge stopped being optional. It was
also the first Android with two SDK releases in one year: the major one in
June, and a minor one, 36.1, in December.

![The Android 16 wordmark: the word Android followed by the number 16, in black](./assets/android-16-logo.svg 'Android 16 logo, shown on a light plate. Wikimedia Commons · Public domain (PD-textlogo) · Trademark of Google LLC')

## At a glance

|                                 |                                                                                   |
| ------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Version                         | Android 16                                                                        |
| API level                       | 36 (36.1 from Android 16 QPR2)                                                    |
| Name                            | Baklava, internal; visible in the SDK constant. Public name: Android 16           |
| `Build.VERSION_CODES`           | `BAKLAVA` (`VERSION_CODES_FULL.BAKLAVA_1` for QPR2)                               |
| Release                         | Pixel rollout began 10 June 2025                                                  |
| Launch devices                  | Samsung Galaxy Z Fold7 and Z Flip7, announced 9 July 2025                         |
| Google Play target-API deadline | New apps and updates from 31 August 2026 (extension available to 1 November 2026) |

Google released Android 16 on 10 June 2025 for the Pixel 6 and newer, including
the Pixel 6a, Pixel Tablet and Pixel Fold. Google's consumer post called it "the
earliest Android has launched a major release in the last few years." The first
AOSP build, `BP2A.250605.031.A2`, carries the June 2025 security patch.

The first phones sold with Android 16 were Samsung's Galaxy Z Fold7 and Z
Flip7, announced on 9 July and on sale from 25 July 2025 with One UI 8. Samsung
says they were the first devices to ship with Android 16 out of the box.

Google confirmed the dessert name Baklava to Android Faithful in October 2024
and said it stays internal. Unlike Android 10 to 12, the name is visible to
developers: the SDK constant is `BAKLAVA`.

## What it brought

- **Progress-centric notifications.** `Notification.ProgressStyle` draws a
  progress bar with `Point` milestones and `Segment` states, for rides,
  deliveries and navigation. Google called it "the basis for Live Updates".
- **Live Updates.** The system promotes some ongoing notifications to the top
  of the shade, the lock screen and a status bar chip. Pixel launched them with
  ride-share and food delivery apps (see [QPR2](#android-16-qpr2-and-the-first-minor-sdk)
  for the public API).
- **Notification grouping.** Notifications from one app are grouped
  automatically, whatever the app does.
- **Adaptive refresh rate.** `Display.hasArrSupport()` and
  `Display.getSuggestedFrameRate(int)` help apps on displays that change their
  refresh rate on the fly. RecyclerView 1.4 uses it when a fling settles.
- **Performance tools.** `ProfilingManager` starts traces by itself on events
  such as a cold start or an ANR. `JobScheduler.getPendingJobReasonsHistory()`
  explains why a job has not run. Games get CPU and GPU headroom APIs.
- **Camera and media.** Hybrid auto-exposure, colour temperature and tint
  controls, night mode detection, motion photo intents, UltraHDR in HEIC, and
  the APV codec (`MediaFormat.MIMETYPE_VIDEO_APV`) for professional video.
- **Photo picker.** It can be embedded in an app's own view hierarchy.
- **Accessibility.** "Outline text" replaces high contrast text, Auracast
  broadcast audio is supported, and LE Audio hearing aid users can talk through
  the phone's microphone in noisy places.
- **Other APIs.** A `RangingManager` for UWB, Bluetooth channel sounding and
  Wi-Fi RTT; AGSL colour filters (`RuntimeColorFilter`); richer haptics in
  `android.os.vibrator`; and a "Measurement system" setting under Languages &
  region.

![A lock screen with a ride-share notification at the top, showing a progress bar with a car icon](./assets/progress-centric-notification.png 'A progress-centric notification on the lock screen, built with ProgressStyle. Android Developers · CC BY 2.5')

The Material 3 Expressive redesign was not in the June release. Google said
more design updates would come later in 2025, and they reached Pixel in the
next quarterly update.

## Security and privacy

- **Advanced Protection.** One switch, under Security & privacy, turns on
  Google's strictest device settings for people at risk, such as journalists.
  It blocks sideloading, turns off 2G and WEP, enables memory tagging (MTE) on
  supported devices, protects USB, reboots a phone locked for 72 hours, and can
  keep encrypted intrusion logs. Apps check it with `AdvancedProtectionManager`
  and the `QUERY_ADVANCED_PROTECTION_MODE` permission.
- **Intent redirection hardening.** Android 16 blocks general intent
  redirection by default, for every app. An app that has to forward a nested
  intent can opt out with `Intent.removeLaunchSecurityProtection()`.
- **Safer intents, opt-in.** With `android:intentMatchingFlags="enforceIntentFilter"`,
  an explicit intent must match the target's intent filter, and intents without
  an action match nothing.
- **Key sharing.** `KeyStoreManager.grantKeyAccess()` lets an app give another
  app, by UID, access to an Android Keystore key.
- **MediaStore version.** `MediaStore.getVersion()` returns a value unique to
  each app; do not parse it or assume its format.
- **GPU syscall filtering.** On Pixel 6 to 9, deprecated and development-only
  Mali GPU IOCTLs are blocked in production builds.

## Permissions

- **Health sensors.** For apps targeting 36, `BODY_SENSORS` gives way to
  granular `android.permissions.health` permissions such as `READ_HEART_RATE`,
  and `BODY_SENSORS_BACKGROUND` to `READ_HEALTH_DATA_IN_BACKGROUND`. Phone apps
  must also show a privacy policy activity, or the permission is revoked.
- **Local network, preview.** Google announced that local network access
  (mDNS, SSDP, `NsdManager`, sockets to private ranges) will need a runtime
  permission. In Android 16 it is opt-in for testing only:
  `adb shell am compat enable RESTRICT_LOCAL_NETWORK <package>`. While the test
  flag is on, `NEARBY_WIFI_DEVICES` restores access.
- **Limited photo access.** For apps targeting 36, photos the app created are
  pre-selected when the user grants partial media access.
- **New permissions for features.** `QUERY_ADVANCED_PROTECTION_MODE` (see
  above). Live Updates gained `POST_PROMOTED_NOTIFICATIONS` in QPR2.

## What changed for your app

### All apps on Android 16

| Change                                                                                                                                       | What to do                                                                              |
| -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| JobScheduler quotas also apply to the `active` bucket, to jobs that outlive the visible app, and to jobs running beside a foreground service | Log `getStopReason()`; use user-initiated data transfer jobs for user-started transfers |
| A job whose `JobParameters` is garbage collected before `jobFinished()` stops with `STOP_REASON_TIMEOUT_ABANDONED`                           | Keep a reference to `JobParameters` and always call `jobFinished()`                     |
| `setImportantWhileForeground()` is ignored                                                                                                   | Remove the call                                                                         |
| Ordered broadcast priority only holds within one process                                                                                     | Coordinate processes through a bound service or AIDL, not `android:priority`            |
| ART internals changed, also on Android 12+ through Play system updates                                                                       | Replace non-SDK interface use; check libraries against Google's known-issues list       |
| A 4 KB-aligned app on a 16 KB page device runs in a compatibility mode and the user sees a dialog                                            | Align native libraries to 16 KB; `android:pageSizeCompat` hides the dialog              |
| `announceForAccessibility()` is deprecated                                                                                                   | Use pane titles, live regions, or `setError()`                                          |
| Intent redirection is blocked by default                                                                                                     | Test flows that launch an intent received from another app                              |
| Companion device discovery no longer times out with `RESULT_DISCOVERY_TIMEOUT`                                                               | Treat `RESULT_USER_REJECTED` as the failure                                             |
| On Bluetooth bond loss, the system keeps the bond, disconnects and asks the user to pair again                                               | Remove your own re-pairing logic                                                        |

### Apps targeting API 36

| Change                                                                                                                        | What to do                                                                                                                 |
| ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| On displays `sw600dp` and wider, `screenOrientation`, `resizeableActivity`, `minAspectRatio` and `maxAspectRatio` are ignored | Build layouts for any size and orientation and save UI state across recreation                                             |
| `windowOptOutEdgeToEdgeEnforcement` is disabled                                                                               | Handle insets; edge-to-edge is mandatory                                                                                   |
| Predictive back is on: `onBackPressed()` is not called and `KEYCODE_BACK` is not dispatched                                   | Move to `OnBackPressedCallback` or `OnBackInvokedCallback`; `android:enableOnBackInvokedCallback="false"` opts out for now |
| `elegantTextHeight` is ignored                                                                                                | Check layouts in Arabic, Thai, Tamil and other affected scripts                                                            |
| `scheduleAtFixedRate` runs at most one missed execution when the app returns                                                  | Do not rely on catch-up runs                                                                                               |
| `BODY_SENSORS` is replaced by health permissions                                                                              | Request the granular permission and declare a privacy policy activity                                                      |
| Bluetooth sends `ACTION_KEY_MISSING` and `ACTION_ENCRYPTION_CHANGE`                                                           | Use them as the bond-loss and bond-restored signals                                                                        |

Games are exempt from the large-screen rule if they declare
`android:appCategory="game"`, and users can still pick the app's old behaviour
in the device's aspect ratio settings. Developers got a temporary opt-out, the
`android.window.PROPERTY_COMPAT_ALLOW_RESTRICTED_RESIZABILITY` property, which
Google said would not apply once an app targets API 37.

Edge-to-edge was already enforced for apps targeting 35 in
[Android 15](/notes/android-15/), which still allowed the opt-out attribute.
Android 16 removed that escape.

## Android 16 QPR2 and the first minor SDK

Android 16 changed Android's release model. Google announced a major SDK
release in Q2 2025, with behaviour changes, and a minor SDK release in Q4 2025
with new APIs only. The quarterly updates in between bring features but no
new SDK.

![A 2025 timeline: 25Q1 and 25Q3 are features only, 25Q2 is the major SDK release with behaviour changes, APIs and features, and 25Q4 is the minor SDK release with APIs and features](./assets/android-2025-release-model.png 'The 2025 release model announced with Android 16. Android Developers · CC BY 2.5')

The minor release is Android 16 QPR2, released on 2 December 2025 to the Pixel 6
and newer, Pixel 10 included. source.android.com lists it as API level 36.1.
Google's post calls it "the first release to utilize a minor SDK version".

`SDK_INT` stays at 36. A new field, `Build.VERSION.SDK_INT_FULL`, carries the
major and minor version. In AOSP the value is the major level times 100,000
plus the minor, so `VERSION_CODES_FULL.BAKLAVA_1` is `3600001`. The source
documents the rule: minor releases add APIs with "no changes gated by
`targetSdkVersion`". `Build.getMajorSdkVersion()` and `getMinorSdkVersion()`
decode the value. Google's own check tests both fields:

```java
if ((Build.VERSION.SDK_INT >= Build.VERSION_CODES.BAKLAVA) &&
    (Build.VERSION.SDK_INT_FULL >= Build.VERSION_CODES_FULL.BAKLAVA_1)) {
  // Call new APIs from the Android 16 QPR2 release
}
```

To compile against 36.1, AGP 8.13.0 or higher accepts a minor level:

```kotlin
android {
    compileSdk {
        version = release(36) { minorApiLevel = 1 }
    }
}
```

What QPR2 added:

- **Live Updates for every app.** `POST_PROMOTED_NOTIFICATIONS` appears in the
  public API at the QPR2 tag, not at 36. An ongoing notification with a title,
  a supported style and `setRequestPromotedOngoing(true)` can be promoted.
  `NotificationManager.canPostPromotedNotifications()` tells whether the user
  allows it.
- **Expanded dark theme.** Users can force dark theme on apps that have none;
  the system reads `isLightTheme` to decide what to invert.
- **Themed icons for all apps.** If an app has no monochrome icon layer, the
  system generates one. Users can also pick icon shapes.
- **SMS OTP protection.** For most apps, a message carrying an SMS Retriever
  hash is held for three hours: the `RECEIVE_SMS` broadcast is withheld and
  SMS provider queries are filtered.
- **Other APIs.** Interactive chooser sessions, developer verification APIs
  for app installs, widget engagement metrics, IAMF audio decoding, and Health
  Connect steps recorded by the device itself.

## What lasted

- **Large screens.** [Android 17](/notes/android-17-stable/) removed the
  temporary opt-out: apps targeting 37 on `sw600dp` displays cannot keep their
  orientation or aspect ratio locks.
- **Local network.** The QPR2 API already lists an `ACCESS_LOCAL_NETWORK`
  permission. Android 17 blocks local network access by default for apps
  targeting 37.
- **SMS OTP.** Android 17 extends the three-hour hold to OTP messages for most
  apps targeting 37.
- **ART.** Google listed a generational Concurrent Mark-Compact collector among
  the QPR2 changes; the [Android 17 note](/notes/android-17-stable/) covers it,
  since ART updates through Google Play system updates.
- **Process.** For Android 17, Google skipped developer previews and went from
  Canary straight to beta; see the [Beta 3 notes](/notes/android-17-beta-3/)
  and the [memory limits note](/notes/android-17-memory-limits/).

## Worth knowing

- The dessert alphabet started over. After Vanilla Ice Cream, Google confirmed
  Baklava, a B, and the first Android 16 build ID starts with B as well:
  `BP2A.250605.031.A2`.
- The Easter egg is the Android 15 starfield, sped up. In AOSP the maximum warp
  went from `10f` with the comment "after all these years" in Android 15 to
  `16f` with "must go faster", and the stars from 34 to 128.
- Holding the logo, or the space bar, warps the stars for five seconds; a
  second later the next stage opens: Landroid, the space game from Android 14,
  now with an Android 16 icon. The activity also asks for an HDR colour mode, "silently ignored on
  displays that don't support HDR color".
- Android 16 was the only 2025 release with planned behaviour changes; Google
  said so in its launch post.
