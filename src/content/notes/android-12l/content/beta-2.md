Beta 2 came out on 12 January 2022, an "incremental beta update" with no new
APIs. Google did not announce it on the Android Developers Blog; the date and
build come from its 12L release notes.

|                |                                                         |
| -------------- | ------------------------------------------------------- |
| Release date   | 12 January 2022                                         |
| Build          | `S2B2.211203.006`                                       |
| Security patch | January 2022                                            |
| Devices        | Pixel 3a, 4, 4a, 5 and 5a series; Android Emulator; GSI |

The fixes Google listed: the lock screen clock was not centred, Recents
showed every app as a black image with "App isn't available", and gesture
navigation sometimes left an image of the previous app over the current one.
Lock screen icons that were too small on large screens and widget bitmaps
that disappeared were fixed too. For the Pixel 6, Google said it was "working
to bring 12L to Pixel 6 soon".

Google's timeline asked developers to start final compatibility testing and
release compatible versions at this milestone. Its overview page still said
APIs finalize "Starting at Beta 2", a line written before Beta 1 shipped
them early.
