Android 12L is the release Google made for large screens. It gave tablets,
foldables and ChromeOS devices a taskbar, drag-and-drop split screen and a
two-column system UI. For developers it brought a new API level, 32,
without a single breaking change, so nothing forced an app to target it.

12L is a feature drop for [Android 12](/notes/android-12/), not a new version
of it. Everything Android 12 changed for apps still applies, and that note
covers it.

![A phone home screen on 12L with a dark wallpaper, the date at the top left, four app icons tinted light blue in the dock and a search bar below them](./assets/android-12l-home-screen.png 'The 12L home screen on a phone-sized emulator. The taskbar and two-column layouts appear only on large screens. Wikimedia Commons · Apache 2.0')

## At a glance

|                                 |                                                                                   |
| ------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Version                         | Android 12L                                                                       |
| API level                       | 32                                                                                |
| Name                            | 12L, public. Internally "S V2" (`Sv2` in the SDK Manager); see below              |
| `Build.VERSION_CODES`           | `S_V2`                                                                            |
| Release                         | Pixel rollout began 7 March 2022                                                  |
| Launch devices                  | Pixel 3a to Pixel 5a first; Pixel 6 and 6 Pro from the April 2022 build           |
| Google Play target-API deadline | None. Google said apps would not have to target 12L, and Play moved from 31 to 33 |

Google announced 12L on 7 March 2022 and released it to AOSP that week. The
first build, `SP2A.220305.012`, went to the Pixel 3a, 4, 4a, 4a (5G), 5 and 5a.
The Pixel 6 and 6 Pro got their first 12L build, `SP2A.220405.004`, with the
April update. For other makers, Google said 12L would reach tablets and foldables from Samsung, Lenovo and Microsoft "later
this year".

Before that came Developer Preview 1 on 27 October 2021, Beta 1 on
8 December 2021 with the final API 32, Beta 2 on 12 January 2022 and Beta 3
on 9 February 2022. Each one has its own part below.

**The name.** Google used "12L" in public and never a dessert. The code says
`S_V2`, its comment says "S V2", AOSP branches are called `android-s-v2-*`, and
the SDK Manager lists the platform as Android 12L ("Sv2"). Android 12 was Snow
Cone, a name Dave Burke confirmed in October 2021. "Snow Cone v2" follows from
it, but neither the code nor Google's documentation uses that name.

## What it brought

- **Two-column system UI.** On large screens, the notification shade shows
  Quick Settings and notifications in two columns. The lock screen uses two
  columns for notifications and the clock, and setup and Settings use the space
  too.
- **Taskbar.** A taskbar on large screens launches and switches apps. Users
  drag an app from it to start split screen, swipe up to go home, and long-press
  to show or hide it.
- **Split screen from Overview.** A new "Split" action in Overview starts split
  screen with one tap.
- **Lock screen controls on the side.** On tablets the PIN and pattern pad sits
  at one side of the screen. Tapping the other side moves it there.
- **Foldables.** A tuned home screen grid and a smoother fold-unfold
  transition between the outer and inner screens.
- **Spatial audio APIs.** `Spatializer`, obtained with
  `AudioManager.getSpatializer()`, reports whether and how the device
  spatializes sound. `AudioAttributes` gained `setSpatializationBehavior()`, and
  `AudioFormat` gained channel masks up to 9.1.6.
- **Accessible drag and drop.** `ACTION_DRAG_START`, `ACTION_DRAG_DROP` and
  `ACTION_DRAG_CANCEL` let an accessibility service start a drag. The view then
  receives `DRAG_FLAG_ACCESSIBILITY_ACTION` and the system drags without
  animations.
- **Foldable cameras.** `CameraCharacteristics.INFO_DEVICE_STATE_SENSOR_ORIENTATION_MAP`
  maps each fold state to the camera sensor orientation, for logical cameras
  whose physical sensors differ.
- **Activity embedding.** Jetpack WindowManager shows two activities side by
  side, such as a list and its detail, with little or no refactoring. It shipped
  as experimental in WindowManager 1.0.0-beta03 on 27 October 2021. It runs on
  large screens with Android 12L or higher whose maker includes the window
  extensions interface.

## Security and privacy

12L added no new security features of its own: Google's list of security
enhancements per release has no 12L entry. It shipped with the fixes of its own
security release notes, which list 56 CVEs: 3 rated High, 52 Moderate and one
with unknown severity. Builds from AOSP default to security patch level 2022-03-01.

## Permissions

One new permission, and apps from the Play Store cannot get it.
`LAUNCH_MULTI_PANE_SETTINGS_DEEP_LINK` is `signature|preinstalled`. It lets a
system or preinstalled app open its own screen inside the two-pane Settings app
with `Settings.ACTION_SETTINGS_EMBED_DEEP_LINK_ACTIVITY`. No runtime permission
changed.

## What changed for your app

Google promised no breaking changes, and 12L has no behaviour-change page of
its own: its overview links to Android 12's. What changed reaches every app on a
large screen, whatever it targets:

| Change                                                                                            | What to do                                                                                                                  |
| ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| The taskbar is reported as an inset, and it can appear or hide at any time                        | Read insets with `ViewCompat.setOnApplyWindowInsetsListener()`; the taskbar counts in `navigationBars()` and `systemBars()` |
| Any app can be dragged into split screen (since Android 12)                                       | Test split screen with windows of several sizes, even with `resizeableActivity="false"`                                     |
| A virtual display from `MediaProjection` is scaled to fit its surface, like `centerInside`        | Check screen recording and casting, also when the device folds                                                              |
| Device makers can customise letterboxing: position left or right, and a redesigned restart button | Use responsive layouts for screens wider than 600dp, and test in compatibility mode                                         |
| From 12L, some large screens ignore an app's fixed orientation and letterbox it                   | Support both orientations, or test what users see                                                                           |
| Play checks apps against its large screen quality guidelines                                      | An app that fails shows large screen users a notice on its listing, and Play adds large-screen ratings                      |

![A portrait news app letterboxed in the middle of a landscape tablet screen, with a taskbar of six app icons along the bottom](./assets/letterboxed-app-taskbar.png 'A portrait app in compatibility mode, with the taskbar below it. Android Developers · CC BY 2.5')

To build against 12L, set `compileSdkVersion` and `targetSdkVersion` to 32.
Use `WindowMetrics` from Jetpack WindowManager instead of
`Display.getRealSize()` to size your layout.

## What lasted

- **Large screen work.** Android 13 says it "builds on" the tablet
  optimizations of 12L: system UI, multitasking and compatibility modes.
- **Letterboxing** kept growing. Android 13 added a dialog that teaches users
  to move a letterboxed app or put it in split screen.
- **Activity embedding** became a larger API in WindowManager 1.1.0 (June
  2023), with `ActivityEmbeddingController` and a manifest property to enable
  splits.
- **API 32 stayed optional.** Play's deadline went from API 31 in 2022 to API
  33 in August 2023. Google's rule counts "major" releases, and 12L was a
  feature drop.
- **Security patches** for 12L ended with Android 12's. The March 2025 Android
  Security Bulletin is the last to list 12L among the updated AOSP versions.

## Worth knowing

- The `S_V2` constant carries a line of Shakespeare in its doc comment: "Once
  more unto the breach, dear friends, once more." It opens Henry V's speech to
  his soldiers before Harfleur, in Act 3, Scene 1.
- On 12L, `Build.VERSION.RELEASE` still says `12`. The build sets
  `PLATFORM_VERSION_LAST_STABLE := 12`, and source.android.com lists 12L as
  version 12, CTS 12.1. Check `SDK_INT >= 32`, never the version string.
- API 32 removed a deprecation. `Environment.getExternalStorageDirectory()`,
  `getExternalStoragePublicDirectory()` and `MediaStore.MediaColumns.DATA`,
  deprecated in Android 10, are no longer deprecated, and they still are not.
- Google counted "over a quarter billion" Android large screen devices, across
  tablets, foldables and ChromeOS, when it announced 12L in October 2021.
