Google released Beta 2 at Google I/O on 11 May 2022. It was the first
Android 13 build offered beyond Pixel: ASUS, HMD (Nokia), Lenovo, OnePlus,
Oppo, Realme, Sharp, Tecno, Vivo, Xiaomi and ZTE ran their own beta programmes
on selected phones, tablets and foldables.

|                    |                                                                         |
| ------------------ | ----------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Release date       | 11 May 2022; Beta 2.1 on 26 May 2022                                    |
| Build              | `TPBB.220414.015`; 2.1 `TPBB.220414.018`                                |
| Security patch     | May 2022                                                                |
| Devices            | Pixel 4 to Pixel 6 Pro; partner devices; Android Emulator; GSI          |
| How to get it      | OTA through Android Beta on Pixel; partners handled their own enrolment |
| Platform stability | No; Google called it "just a step away"                                 |

Three developer features arrived: the ahead-of-time back APIs behind
predictive back, a resource file that lists an app's supported languages
(`android:localeConfig`), and `USE_EXACT_ALARM`, the install-time permission
for alarm clocks and calendars.

Beta 2.1 fixed crashes when turning on the hotspot or disconnecting from
Android Auto, a blank list of search suggestions, and a dialing sound heard
during calls.
